Z
THE DESIGNER
ZHAREAL POPA
Creative Director & Founder

Zhareal Popa has a degree in Apparel Design from Oregon State University — but her real education started when she stopped making clothes for people and started making them for herself. What began as alterations turned into full collections: each one darker, more intentional, more her. She designs for women who don't fit a mold and don't want to. Every piece she makes is handbuilt, hand-finished, and made to be felt.

Z
TECH & FASHION · BUILDER
ZAKIA YESMIN
Tech & Fashion · Builder

Zakia Yesmin is the person who makes sure ZHADORE has a digital heartbeat. She studies Business Information Systems in New York and lives at the intersection of tech and fashion — the place most people don't think to look. She built this website. She builds the strategy. She's here because she believes the most powerful thing a creative brand can have is someone who understands both the art and the code behind it.
