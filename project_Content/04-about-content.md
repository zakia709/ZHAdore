  # Extended brand origin story (can be used on a dedicated /about page in future)
  origin_story: >-
    ZHADORE was born at the intersection of craft and code — two women, 
    two disciplines, one vision. Zhareal Popa had been designing garments 
    since she could hold a needle, earning her degree in Apparel Design 
    from Oregon State University. Zakia Yesmin had been building digital 
    experiences, studying Business Information Systems in New York. 

    They met through a shared belief: that fashion had lost its soul to 
    mass production, and that the future belonged to brands that understood 
    both the art of the garment AND the code behind the brand.

    ZHADORE is the answer. Every piece is handbuilt by Zhareal. Every 
    pixel and strategy is crafted by Zakia. Together, they're proving 
    that a two-woman operation can move with the precision and impact 
    of a much larger house — because they're not just running a brand. 
    They're building an empire, one intentional stitch at a time.

    I have a degree in Apparel Design from Oregon State University — 
    but my real education started the moment I realized every woman 
    deserves something made for her alone. What began as creating 
    one-of-one pieces for others became full collections: each one 
    darker, and yet — the deeper the shadow, the more deliberately 
    the light finds its way in. I design for women who don't fit a 
    mold and don't want to. My goal is to create artwork for the 
    one-of-one woman — she who values authenticity, who knows what 
    hard work costs, and who deserves to be preserved in the form 
    of fashion. Every piece is handbuilt, hand-finished, and made 
    to be felt.

  # Bio — EXTENDED VERSION (for press kit / about page expansion)
  bio_extended: >-
    Zhareal Popa is the creative force behind ZHADORE. With a formal 
    education in Apparel Design from Oregon State University and years 
    of hands-on experience creating one-of-one pieces for private clients, 
    she brings a rare combination of technical precision and artistic 
    fearlessness to every garment.

    Her design philosophy is rooted in the belief that clothing should 
    preserve the wearer — not just cover her. Each ZHADORE piece begins 
    with the body it's meant for, built outward with intention, finished 
    by hand, and designed to carry meaning far beyond a single season.

    Zhareal does not design for trends. She designs for women who know 
    who they are and want their clothing to say the same.
